## 1. Wert&Co — wertco.com

**What they are:** The design community's best-known retained executive search firm (founded 1995, NYC, ~40 staff). Places permanent senior/leadership design roles — Founding Designer through CDO — at companies like Airbnb, Google, and Dropbox. **They do not place contract/freelance work.** Treat as a multi-year leadership-track relationship, not a gig source.

**Intake:** Email-based, no signup form.
- Candidates: **candidates@wertco.com** (general: conversations@wertco.com) — [wertco.com/contact](https://www.wertco.com/contact/)

**People:**
- **Judy Wert** — Co-Founder. The marquee name in design exec search; TechCrunch profiled her as the recruiter who finds "the top 1% of designers." [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/judywert). Email not public (aggregators suggest first-name@wertco.com — unverified).
- **Daniel Wert** — CEO since ~2023. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/qwertyresume/)
- **Jeff Wert** — Co-Founder. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeff-wert-868876b/)

**How to get in:** Famously selective and referral-driven. Send a tight intro + portfolio to candidates@wertco.com, connect with Judy/Daniel on LinkedIn, and cultivate for future VP/CDO mandates.

## 2. Aquent (Aquent Talent) — aquent.com

**What they are:** The largest global creative/design staffing firm — freelance, contract, temp-to-hire, and perm across 8 countries. ~10,000 talent deployed annually; W-2 employment with strong contractor benefits in the US. - **Rohshann "Ro" Pilla** — President, Aquent Talent. [Bio](https://aquent.com/leadership/rohshann-pilla). Email not public.
- **John H. Chuang** — Founder & CEO. [Bio](https://aquent.com/leadership/john-chuang)

**How to get in:** Apply to specific live roles rather than cold-registering. Vetting includes video interview, portfolio review, skills assessments, and reference checks. Keep the MyAquent portfolio link current; Agents scrutinize LinkedIn/title accuracy closely.

## 3. A.Team — a.team

**What they are:** Vetted team-formation platform placing senior product designers (alongside engineers/PMs) into cross-functional "missions" for clients like Lyft, PepsiCo, and The Economist. Contract only; engagements skew long (12–18 months). Senior product designers run roughly $80–$155/hr; **<2% acceptance rate**. Remote-first, US-market-centric.

## 7. Toptal — toptal.com

**What they are:** Elite global freelance marketplace ("top 3%"), fully remote, contract only. Design vertical covers UI, UX, product, and design directors. Platform-mediated matching — **there are no public recruiters to lobby; the screening pipeline is the only door.** <3% of ~200,000 annual applicants accepted.

**Intake:**
- Apply: [toptal.com/talent/apply](https://www.toptal.com/talent/apply)
- Designer screening stages: (1) English/communication interview → (2) portfolio review + live case-study presentation → (3) live screening exercises → (4) test project (1–3 weeks). Pipeline runs 3–8 weeks; rejection triggers a 6-month reapplication wait.

**People (networking surface, not placement contacts):**
- **Community Leaders program** — regional design community moderators: [toptal.com/community](https://www.toptal.com/community)
- ⚠️ Danielle Reid (built Toptal's design vertical) left in 2019 — do not contact.

**How to get in:** Take the English/communication interview seriously (it filters many). Prepare 2–3 polished case studies with articulated process and business outcomes. Don't rush the test project — polish beats speed. A referral link from a current Toptal designer smooths entry.
